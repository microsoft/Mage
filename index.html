Models

Mage is a family of lightweight, research-friendly multimodal models built at a 4B-parameter scale, spanning visual understanding, generation, and computer use. Mage-VL and Mage-Flow share a codec-aligned efficiency philosophy—spend representation capacity where the signal is. Mage-VL-Cowork extends the family to desktop workflows, learning to combine graphical interaction and programmatic execution in one compact policy.
